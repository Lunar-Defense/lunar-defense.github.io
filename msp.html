What the app does

Real security checks, in plain language.

Every client runs the same one-tap sweep: a battery of on-device checks that inspect the iPhone and its network connection, then combine into one honest verdict. The analysis runs entirely on the device, and the client's results and personal information stay on it.

One-tap sweep

Tap once and the app runs a full battery of security checks on the device in seconds, with no setup for the client.

Device integrity

Checks for jailbreak traces, injected code, broken app isolation and debuggers, and verifies the iPhone is genuine with Apple.

Connection safety

Detects proxies and network interception, and confirms the app's own connection can't be quietly impersonated or watched.

Phishing and QR checks

The client can check a suspicious message or scan a QR code and get a plain verdict before they act on it.

Correlation cross-check

A final pass weighs every signal together, catching patterns that line up into a bigger picture no single check would see.

A verdict, not a score

One plain status, Protected, Needs attention, or Action needed, earned by named evidence and paired with a ranked list of what to do next.

When a client needs help

On-demand context, and proof of what happened.

Technician-facing log view

One tap swaps the client's friendly view for a full typed event timeline with timestamps, built for a live screen-share support session.

Chain-integrity badge

One glance answers "can I trust this device's history?" The badge reads Verified, or one of several honestly-labeled failure states.

Disclosure logging

Opening the technician view writes a permanent record into the client's own log stating their information was viewed, and when.

Audit-log health banner

A non-dismissible banner surfaces on the client's home screen if logging itself has stopped or been tampered with. It stays silent when healthy.

Tamper-evident, hash-chained

Frequently asked

Who is Lunar Defense Managed for?+

IT and security providers who support a base of iPhone users. You deploy and license it; your clients get the full security app, with one-tap sweeps and automatic scans, for free, and you get a tamper-evident record built for support.

How does a client get it?+

They download the free app and enter the activation code you issued. Until it's activated the app is inert. It's explicitly presented as provided by their IT or security provider.

Is it free for the client?+

Yes. Everything is free to the client, including the Pro automation (background scans, push alerts, and instant notification) with no paywall or upsell. You hold the seat license.

What does the audit log record?+

Verdicts and indicators only (a finding that appears, escalates, or clears), plus disclosures when a technician opens the log view. It never records message content or full URLs, and a clean day writes nothing. It's encrypted and hash-chained, so tampering is detected and preserved rather than hidden.

Does it work without connectivity?+

Yes. A 21-day token is verified fully offline at every launch, so a client on a jobsite or without a connection keeps working uninterrupted for up to three weeks.
