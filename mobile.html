Frequently asked

Is Lunar Defense Mobile Security free?+

Yes. Manual sweeps are completely free, forever, no account required. A Pro subscription adds automatic background sweeps and alerts when something changes.

Do my scan results ever leave my device?+

No. The analysis runs on your device and your results stay there. The app makes only a few network connections — verifying your device is genuine with Apple, checking that the connection to us isn't being intercepted, push notifications for background scans, and payments through Apple — and none of them carry your scan results or personal information. The one exception is one you control: if you tap "Get help," the app drafts an email containing your findings, and nothing is sent until you read it and send it yourself. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

What does a sweep check?+

Three families of checks plus a final cross-check: device integrity (operating-system tampering, unauthorized code running alongside the app, device genuineness), connection safety (interception and impersonation), and a correlation step that weighs everything together. Each finding is explained in plain language.

Will it remove a threat it finds?+

Lunar Defense is a security checkup and advisor, not an antivirus. It detects issues and gives you a clear, ranked list of what to do, safety-first steps first. It doesn't quarantine or delete files.

What do I get with Pro?+

Automatic background sweeps on a schedule, plus alerts when something changes, so you stay covered without manually running a sweep. $4.99/month or $49.99/year with a 3-day free trial.

How do I cancel my subscription?+

Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID. You can cancel anytime in Settings on your iPhone or iPad, under your name → Subscriptions. You keep Pro until the end of your billing period.
